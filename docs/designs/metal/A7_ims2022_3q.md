# IMS 2022 workshop chip (Metal A.7)

Three pocket transmons in a triangle, all coupled to each other through three λ/2 buses. Two are read out through
interdigital tees on a short north feedline; the third has its own series capacitor and bond pad. It is the chip of
the Qiskit Metal IMS 2022 workshop (Apache-2.0, © IBM and contributors), in the state the tutorial reaches after
tuning. The Quantcore rebuild is distributed under GPL-3.0.

Build this one to see all-to-all coupling of three qubits and the design targets the tutorial states (qubit
frequencies near 4.8, 5.0 and 5.2 GHz). It fits inside the standard 7.5 mm Quantcore die.

1. **Die.** The 7.5 mm die with the QCT frame, and the tutorial's targets attached.
2. **Bond pads.** Three launchpads (two north, one south), slid to the 100 µm pad-edge rule (the picture zooms on one pad).
3. **Feedline.** The north feedline with two interdigital tees, and the third qubit's series capacitor.
4. **Qubits.** Three Metal pocket transmons, their junctions sized for the targets.
5. **Readout resonators.** Three λ/2 serpentines of the tutorial's tuned lengths.
6. **Coupling.** Three λ/2 buses, all-to-all.
7. **Control lines.** None; the qubits are fixed-frequency and driven through their readouts.
8. **Whole chip.** Sim groups for the FEM jobs, the content centred in the die, the design saved.

## Build spec

The rest of this page is the detailed spec the notebook was built from: every component, port, route and length.

> The `metal:` references in this spec build as written, through the `metal:` wrapper (ports are the Metal pin names, parameters the Metal options). The Quantcore and KQC substitutions are optional.

Source: Quantum Metal `docs/circuit-examples/full-design-flow-examples/A.7-IMS-2022-workshop.ipynb`. Licence
Apache-2.0.
The chip has three `TransmonPocket6` qubits in a triangle, all-to-all coupled by three λ/2 bus meanders. The two
north qubits are read out through `CapNInterdigitalTee` taps on a short shared feedline; Q_2 reads out through a
series `CapNInterdigital` to its own pad. The notebook states full design targets and tunes Q_0, Q_1, Bus_01 and
Bus_02 with Q3D-LOM and HFSS-EPR sweeps.
Chip 6000 × 6000 µm, centred, so **Designer = Metal + (3000, 3000)**. CPW 10/6 µm, fillet 99 µm. The junctions are
FakeJunction_01 cells. The GDS is exported with cheesing (3 µm holes, 50 µm pitch).
A first build uses these substitutions: `Xmon` for TransmonPocket6 (3 claws each), hanger `end="open"` (or a KQC
splitter plus cap) for the interdigital tee, `FingerCapacitorSquare`, `BondPad`.

### Launchers, pads, test structures, markers, labels
3 pads: the feedline in/out at the north (x 1000 and 5000) and the Q_2 readout at the south. There are no markers.
Fabrication GDS uses `cheese` on layer 1 with a no-cheese buffer of 40 µm.

### LOM node structure
- Q_0, Q_1 and Q_2 are islands with JJs, all-to-all coupled through λ/2 buses (C_g at each claw).
- R0 and R1 are λ/2 resonators, C_g at the claw and C_κ (tee) to FL, which runs between two 50 Ω ports.
- R2 is λ/2, with C_g at the claw and a series C to its 50 Ω port.
- There are no control lines.

### Targets (if stated)

The common targets are anharmonicity 300 MHz, qubit–bus g 80 MHz, qubit–readout χ 1 MHz, readout Q_ext 2000 and bus
frequencies 5.8 / 6.0 / 6.2 GHz (Bus_01, Bus_02, Bus_12). The Bus_02 sweep tried 9.4, 9.3 and 9.2 mm and gave
9.35 mm.

### Simplifications for a first rebuild
1. Use Xmon claws for the TransmonPocket6 pads, and use the LOM loop (`d.targets(...)`) to retune the claw sizes and
   Lj toward the table instead of copying the pocket pad sizes.
2. The interdigital tee is a hanger `end="open"`. For fidelity, put a KQC `WaveguideCoplanarSplitter` (ports a/b/c)
   in FL and a `FingerCapacitorSquare` on port c.
3. Keep the tuned bus lengths (9550 / 9350 / 9700) as the starting point.

### Quantcore PDK rebuild

The notebook is a Quantcore PDK rebuild of the source, not a replica: the netlist, LOM and imported components are kept, and the process is Quantcore's.

Notebook: [`examples/designs/metal/a7_ims2022_3q.ipynb`](../../../examples/designs/metal/a7_ims2022_3q.ipynb).

- Chip: the Quantcore die frame (L dicing markers 1000/0, 3 × 3 alignment grids 1001/0, chip boundary 100/0, device ID where an edge is free; any brand text is QCT), on the 7.5 × 7.5 mm Quantcore die; the source's 6 × 6 mm chip area is centred in it (`box=(-750, -750, 6750, 6750)`), so every coordinate stays the source's.
- Stack: sapphire 650 µm, εr 10.25 (the PDK default stack, used by the LOM estimates and every FEM export); Metal's `DesignPlanar` default is silicon (εr 11.45, 750 µm), so the LOM capacitances, resonator frequencies and FEM jobs here are for sapphire rather than the source's substrate.
- Junctions: Quantcore Nb/AlOx/Nb trilayer junctions (`junction_type` from `layouts.registry.trilayer_junction`): fingers on 2/0 + 4/0, spacer 50/0 with the 51–53/0 gradient, sized on the PDK junction rule (Nb Δ = 1.4 meV, I_c·R_n = πΔ/2e, R_n·A 13.5 kΩ·µm² and C_J 50 fF/µm² assumed). Each qubit is sized for the tutorial's target f_q (4.8 / 5.0 / 5.2 GHz) with its C_Σ (≈ 49–56 fF) from the LOM: straight fingers 0.22 / 0.23 / 0.23 µm × 0.6 µm, L_J 15.3 / 14.6 / 14.6 nH, C_J 6.6–6.9 fF, so R_n ≈ 98–104 kΩ, above the 40–50 kΩ window. These pockets are small (a 45 kΩ junction would put them at ≈ 7.0–7.3 GHz, still inside 4–8 GHz). Cell 1 prints the sizing.
- Kept: the topology and every Metal component with the source's options (through the `metal:` wrapper), positions and orientations, every route's end pins and total length, the sim groups (buses 9550 / 9350 / 9700 µm, readouts 8800 / 8500 / 8300 µm, all stated targets).
